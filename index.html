Why MindSpace exists

Most of us don't need another complicated app — we need three quiet minutes to notice how we're doing. MindSpace was built as a simple, judgment-free companion for that: track a mood, jot a thought, and get a small nudge in the right direction.

There's no sign-up, no server, and no one reading your entries. Everything you write is kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. Clearing your browser data will remove it, so consider that your own private notebook.

  • Built with plain HTML, CSS & JavaScript
  • No tracking, ads, or accounts
  • Works fully offline once loaded
